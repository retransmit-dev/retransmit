# Domains

Retransmit only sends email from domains that are registered on your account
and verified.

## Adding a domain

1. Open **Domains** in the dashboard.
2. Enter your domain, e.g. `yourdomain.com`.
3. Pick the region the domain sends from (see below).
4. Choose a Return-Path subdomain. The default `mail` gives `mail.yourdomain.com`.
5. Publish the DNS records shown at your DNS provider.
6. Wait for verification to complete, then check the status from the domain's details.

## Regions

Email is sent out of the region a domain was verified in. The region cannot be
changed after the domain is added; delete the domain and add it again to move it.

| Region | Location |
| --- | --- |
| `eu-central-1` | Frankfurt |
| `us-east-1` | N. Virginia |
| `ap-southeast-1` | Singapore |
| `af-south-1` | Cape Town |

Pick the region closest to most of your recipients, or the one your data
residency rules require.

## Return-Path

The Return-Path is the address bounces and delivery reports go back to. Using
a subdomain you control, instead of the provider's default, aligns SPF with
your domain and keeps your DMARC policy intact.

It must be a subdomain of the sending domain. The root domain is not accepted.

## DNS records

| Record | Name | Purpose | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CNAME` (x3) | `<token>._domainkey.yourdomain.com` | DKIM signing | Yes |
| `MX` | `mail.yourdomain.com` | Return-Path, routes bounces back to the sending region | Yes |
| `TXT` | `mail.yourdomain.com` | SPF for the Return-Path (`v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all`) | Yes |
| `TXT` | `_dmarc.yourdomain.com` | DMARC policy | Recommended |

The exact values are shown in the dashboard and can be copied one by one.

## Domain statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | DNS records not yet detected. Propagation can take a few hours. |
| `verified` | Ready to send. |
| `failed` | Verification failed. Re-check the DNS records. |
| `temporary_failure` | Verification hit a transient issue and will be retried. |

## Sending from an unready domain

`POST /v1/emails` rejects the request before anything is sent:

| Condition | Response |
| --- | --- |
| The `from` domain is not on your account | `403 domain_not_found` |
| The domain exists but is not `verified` | `403 domain_not_verified`. The message includes the current status. |

```json
{
  "error": {
    "code": "domain_not_verified",
    "message": "The domain `yourdomain.com` is not verified yet (status: pending)."
  }
}
```

> [!TIP]
>
> `from` accepts a bare address (`hello@yourdomain.com`) or a display name
> form (`Acme <hello@yourdomain.com>`). The domain is taken from the address.
