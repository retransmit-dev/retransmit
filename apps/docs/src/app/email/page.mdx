# Email

Send transactional or marketing email from verified domains, with batch
sending and delivery tracking.

## Get started

- [Send an email](/email/send): queue a single email.
- [Send a batch](/email/send-batch): queue up to 10,000 emails at once.
- [Get an email](/email/get): status and event history.
- [Get a batch](/email/get-batch): aggregate batch progress.

## Configure email

- [Domains](/email/domains): register and verify a sending domain.
- [Marketing Email](/email/marketing-email): unsubscribe handling and suppression.
- [Migrate from Resend](/email/migrate-from-resend): swap the client and render React Email templates to HTML.

For authentication, response formats, and shared behavior, see the
[API Reference](/api-reference).
