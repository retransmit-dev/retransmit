# Marketing Email

Cold outreach, newsletters, and campaigns need a working unsubscribe path —
mailbox providers require it (Gmail and Yahoo enforce one-click unsubscribe
for bulk senders), and honoring it protects your domain reputation.

Set `marketing: true` on a send and Retransmit handles all of it:

1. **A link in your body** — the `{{{unsubscribe_url}}}` placeholder anywhere
   in your `html` or `text` is replaced with a hosted unsubscribe link. Put it
   in your footer.
2. **One-click unsubscribe at the top** — the message is sent with
   `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058), so Gmail,
   Yahoo, and others show their own **Unsubscribe** action next to the sender
   name. No body markup needed; this happens automatically.
3. **Suppression** — recipients who unsubscribe land on your organization's
   suppression list, and later marketing sends to them are skipped
   automatically.

## Sending a marketing email

<Tabs items={["Node.js", "cURL"]}>
<Tabs.Tab>
```ts
import { Retransmit } from "retransmit.dev";

const retransmit = new Retransmit(process.env.RETRANSMIT_API_KEY);

const { data, error } = await retransmit.emails.send({
  from: "Acme <hello@yourdomain.com>",
  to: "prospect@example.com",
  subject: "A quicker way to handle invoices",
  marketing: true,
  html: `
    <p>Hi — saw you're hiring ops folks and thought this might help...</p>
    <p style="color:#888;font-size:12px">
      Don't want these emails? <a href="{{{unsubscribe_url}}}">Unsubscribe</a>.
    </p>
  `,
  text: "Hi — saw you're hiring ops folks...\n\nUnsubscribe: {{{unsubscribe_url}}}",
});
```
</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>
```sh
curl -X POST https://api.retransmit.dev/v1/emails \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $RETRANSMIT_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "from": "Acme <hello@yourdomain.com>",
    "to": "prospect@example.com",
    "subject": "A quicker way to handle invoices",
    "marketing": true,
    "html": "<p>Hi...</p><p><a href=\"{{{unsubscribe_url}}}\">Unsubscribe</a></p>"
  }'
```
</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

The placeholder must be written exactly as `{{{unsubscribe_url}}}` (triple
braces, lowercase). It works in both `html` and `text`, as many times as you
like. On non-marketing sends the placeholder is left untouched and no headers
are added.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> Send marketing email to **one recipient per email** (use
> [Send Batch](/api-reference/send-batch) for volume). The unsubscribe link is
> per-email, so an email with several `to` recipients would unsubscribe all of
> them at once — and every recipient could see the others.

## What the recipient sees

The body link opens a confirmation page showing their address and an
**Unsubscribe** button; the unsubscribe only happens when they press it, so
corporate link scanners that follow every URL in an email can't unsubscribe
people by accident. The provider's one-click action (the top-of-message
unsubscribe) skips the page and takes effect immediately, as RFC 8058
requires.

## After an unsubscribe

- The address is added to your suppression list with reason `unsubscribe`.
- Future sends with `marketing: true` to that address are skipped. If every
  recipient of an email is suppressed, the email gets status `suppressed`
  instead of being sent.
- **Transactional sends are not blocked.** Someone who opts out of your
  campaigns still gets their receipts and password resets. Bounce, complaint,
  and manual suppressions block everything.
- An `email.unsubscribed` [webhook](/webhooks) fires with the unsubscribed
  addresses in `data.data.addresses`, so you can sync opt-outs back to your
  CRM.

## Deliverability checklist for cold outreach

- Verify your sending domain and keep DMARC aligned ([Domains](/domains)).
- Always include the `{{{unsubscribe_url}}}` link in the body, even though the
  headers are added for you — many recipients look for it in the footer, and
  regulations like CAN-SPAM and GDPR expect a visible opt-out.
- Identify yourself honestly: real sender name, real reply address, and your
  postal address in the footer where the law you operate under requires it.
- Warm up new domains slowly and keep an eye on `email.bounced` and
  `email.complained` webhooks — spikes there tank deliverability fast.
